Runner Alerts — how it works

Following someone at one of our races? Get a text each time they cross a timing checkpoint, straight from the timing system.

Signing up

  1. Open the runner's page on the race's live results site (linked from the race page on race day).
  2. In the “Text me when #… crosses a checkpoint” box, enter your own mobile number. The bib is filled in from the page.
  3. Tap Text me updates. You'll get a confirmation text within a few seconds.

The exact form, with the consent language under the button:

What you'll receive

  • Happily Running: You're subscribed to alerts for bib 42 (Virgil Crest 100). You'll get a text at each checkpoint. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
  • Happily Running: Bib 42 passed Hurt Locker at 2:14 PM, mile 47.1 of 100. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
  • Happily Running: Bib 42 FINISHED Virgil Crest 100 at 9:52 AM Sunday, 27:52:10. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Typically 3–8 messages over a race. Nothing before or after, and never marketing.

Stopping

Reply STOP to any message and you're removed immediately. Reply HELP for help. All subscriptions end automatically when the race closes and numbers are deleted.
