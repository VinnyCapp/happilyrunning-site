About Us

The Mark

About the logo.

Two words, two weights. "Happily" comes down in ink — solid, grounded, the state of mind. "Running" lifts up in moss green — what you actually do with it. The order matters. The happy comes first. The running is how we get there.

The dashed trail. That broken line beneath the wordmark is a trail blaze — the kind you follow through the woods when you've got miles to go and only the next mark to aim for. It's dashed because a trail isn't a straight shot. You lose it. You find it. You keep going.

The dot. That single dot on the trail? That's you. Somewhere in the middle of it, moving forward. Not at the start, not at the finish — just running your race, right now.

"How are you running?" The tagline in the JetBrains Mono underline. It's the question we ask each other at aid stations, at mile 80 of a hundred, at the finish with a beer in hand. It's not "how fast?" — it's "how?" How are you doing it? That's what we care about.
