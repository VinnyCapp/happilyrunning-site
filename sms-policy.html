Runner Alerts — SMS Terms & Privacy

Runner Alerts is an optional text-message service on our live results pages. Follow a runner, get a text each time they cross a timing checkpoint. This page is the fine print.

What the program is

Runner Alerts sends an automated text each time a runner you choose to follow is recorded at a race timing checkpoint. Each message contains the runner's bib number, the checkpoint name, the time of crossing and the course mileage, and may include a finish notification. Messages are sent only while the race is in progress.

How you opt in

You opt in by entering your own mobile number and a runner's bib number on that runner's page at our live results website and tapping “Text me updates”. You'll get a confirmation text right away. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. We never add numbers from registration data or any other source — only numbers entered on the form.

Message frequency and cost

Frequency varies by race and runner, typically 3–8 messages over a single race day or weekend. Message and data rates may apply according to your carrier's plan. Happily Running does not charge for this service.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving alerts immediately. Reply HELP for help, or email vinny@happilyrunning.com. Subscriptions also end automatically when the race closes.

Privacy

Your mobile number is used only to deliver the alerts you requested and is kept only for the duration of the race, after which it is deleted. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS consent with any third party, and mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Messages are delivered through our SMS provider (Twilio) solely to transmit the alerts. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
