Volunteer

Races don't run themselves.

Every cowbell. Every PB&J. Every flag in the ground. All of it takes people who show up. Volunteers are the reason our races feel the way they do.

What we need

Aid station captains, aid station crew, course marshals, finish-line help, packet pickup, parking direction, trail marking, post-race cleanup, medical support. Short shifts, long shifts, full days — whatever you can give, we'll find a spot.

How it works

Every volunteer shift earns you credits. Rack up enough credits and you can redeem them for a free entry to a future race. The bigger the race you want, the more credits it takes. Simple.

+ Good food. Better company. A volunteer shirt.

Credit Redemption

What your credits are worth.

One shift = one credit. Use them how you want, when you want. Credits don't expire.
