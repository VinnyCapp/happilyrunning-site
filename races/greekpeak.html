The Festival

Seasoned trail runner or first-timer, there's something on the mountain for you. Courses weave through woodland paths and climb the kind of hills that reward you with views of the surrounding peaks. Fun challenge, every skill level.

A true family affair. After the finish line, the mountain turns into a playground — mountain coaster, indoor waterpark, and Trax Pub & Grill at the base with a massive outdoor deck.

Distances

25K ⛰️

Twenty-five kilometers of mountain trail. You'll go up. You'll come down. You'll go up again. The exact course is TBD, but we promise you'll earn that finish-line beverage.

10K 🏃

A mountain-ish 10K. Two loops of the 5K course. It's not flat, it's not easy, but it's doable. At least one water stop near the top of the course.

5K 👟

A mountain-ish 5K. While it's only 3.1 miles, it's not flat, it's not easy — but it's doable for sure.

Uphill 1K 📈

Straight up Iliad, Greek Peak's steepest slope. 1K distance. 1K feet of gain. Get it. This is the final event of the weekend — run it fresh, or save your juice for one last push after another race.

Doggo 5K 🐶

A canicross-style 5K with your dog. All dogs must be well-behaved, on leash, and under handler control. Muzzles allowed; face leads, flexi-leashes, and no-pull harnesses are not. Reactive dogs will be directed to a special parking area.

Night 10K / Night 5K 🌙

Same as the day versions. Just in the dark. Headlamp required.

1 Mile Kids Run 🧒 — Free (12 & under)

We want kids in on the fun. Course is well marked. No specific age for running alongside — if you're not comfortable with them running alone, go with them.

Après-Race

When the running's done, the celebration moves to the base of the mountain. Trax Pub & Grill, local craft beer, and a deck that was built for swapping stories about the hills you just climbed.
