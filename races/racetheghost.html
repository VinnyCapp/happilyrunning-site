Halloween 2026 · Dryden, NY

Race the Ghost

A Halloween 5K out and back on the Dryden Rail Trail. Costumes encouraged. Ice cream guaranteed. Start and finish at Dryden Creamery — our partners for the weekend.

The race

5K out and back from Dryden Creamery on the Dryden Rail Trail. Flat, fast, easy. Run it, walk it, push a stroller, bring the dog. If the start time lands late enough that we need them, course markings will glow.

What's included

A finisher cone from Dryden Creamery. That's the bib pickup. That's the post-race fuel. That's most of why we do this.

Plus a race bib, the costume contest, and bragging rights at the creamery for the rest of the year.

Costume contest

Awards in five categories, judged after the finish:

  • 👻 Spookiest
  • 🧙🏾‍♂️ Best solo
  • 🧛🏼‍♀️🧟🏽 Best couple
  • 🎃 Best group
  • ✨ Most creative
