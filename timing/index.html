Timing · Event Production · Consulting

Race timing, from a box of safety pins to a fully produced event.

Happily Running is a family-run race timing, event production and consulting company based in Dryden, NY. We time road, trail and ultra events from 5K to 150 miles, and we run our own races too, so we know what a race director needs on race day.

Services

Three ways we work with race directors.

Race timing

Accurate RFID chip timing and fast, public results for your event.

What's included

  • RFID chip timing with ChronoTrack hardware: bib chips, mats and readers at the start, finish and any split points
  • Printed race bibs with chips encoded and assigned to each runner
  • Gun and net (chip) times, splits, overall, gender and age-group results
  • Live results online as runners cross the mat, plus final results posted to your registration platform
  • Works with any registration platform: UltraSignup, RunSignup, Race Roster, OpenSplitTime and others
  • Custom software when you want it: branded live-results pages, runner tracking, SMS alerts for crews and family, race-day dashboards and lifetime stats

Event production

We plan and run the whole event, or the parts you don't want to handle yourself.

What's included

  • Event planning, timelines and race-day schedule
  • Course design, marking and course-marshal plans
  • Permits, insurance and venue coordination guidance
  • Start/finish setup, aid stations and volunteer staffing plans
  • Packet pickup, awards and post-race close-out
  • Sponsor activation support for charity and community events

Consulting

Advice for race directors who want to run their own event well.

What's included

  • Timing setup: what hardware you need, where mats and split points go, and how results flow
  • Registration: choosing a platform, pricing tiers, waivers and data exports for timing
  • Course planning: layout, measurement, aid-station spacing and cut-offs
  • Race-day operations: staffing, communications, safety and contingency plans
  • A review of past events to find what to fix next year
How we work with your event

Any size of event. The help fits the event.

We work with events of any size, from a first-year charity 5K to a multi-day 150-mile ultra. What that help looks like depends on what your event needs:

  • On-site timing crew. Our team brings the equipment, sets up, times the race and posts results.
  • Remote support with your own volunteers. We prepare the timing setup and results, train your volunteers, and support them from off-site on race day.
  • A referral when it isn't us. If we can't do it ourselves, we refer you to someone who can.
Service area

Where we work.

Our home region is New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut. We are based in Dryden, NY, in the Finger Lakes, and regularly work events from Central and Western New York to Long Island. For larger events we travel nationally.

Pricing

Every event is quoted individually.

Race timing and production costs depend on the event. The main factors in a quote are:

  • Field size (number of participants)
  • Number of timing points (start, finish and splits)
  • Distance and duration of the event
  • Travel to the event
  • On-site crew or remote support
  • Production scope beyond timing

Ask for a quote with your date, distances and expected field size.

FAQ

Race timing questions.

Do you time small races?

Yes. We time races of every size, including first-year 5Ks with a few dozen runners. Small events get the same chip timing and live results as our largest ultras.

Do you travel?

Yes. Our home region is New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut, and we travel nationally for larger events. Travel is one of the factors in your quote.

What does race timing cost?

Every event is quoted individually. The price depends on field size, number of timing points, distance and duration, travel, on-site or remote support, and production scope. Send us your event details and we will send a quote.

Which registration platforms do you work with?

We work with any registration platform, including UltraSignup, RunSignup, Race Roster and OpenSplitTime. We import your participant list for timing and post final results back to your platform.

Do you provide live results?

Yes. Results go online live as runners cross the timing mats, so runners, crews and families can follow along. We can also build a branded live-results page and text alerts for your event.

Can you time a 100-mile or multi-day event?

Yes. We time 100-mile and multi-day ultras, including our own Tesla Hertz Run with distances up to 150 miles over four days. Our timing system handles overnight splits, loop counts and date changes.

Can you help if I have my own timing gear?

Yes. We can consult on setup, train your team, run your results remotely, or add our equipment where you need more timing points. If your setup needs something we don't offer, we will refer you to someone who can help.
